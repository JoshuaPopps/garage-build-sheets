Your bike. Your mods. Your photos. Your notes. Offline, no account, free.

Welcome to the garage

Three example bikes are loaded so you can see how a build sheet works — tap around, move a part from Planned to Installed, hide the money, try a different sheet style. Nothing here is yours yet, and the examples are labelled Example everywhere.

When you’re ready, add your own bike — it takes about 30 seconds — or clear the examples and start with an empty garage.

Your garage

No bikes in the garage yet.

Here’s the whole idea, in three steps:

  1. Add a bike — year, make, model, and what you’re building it into.
  2. Add the parts you fit, buy, or are dreaming about — each one gets a stage: Idea, Planned, Purchased, Installed.
  3. Add photos and notes as you go. The app turns it into a stylised build sheet you can print or share.

Everything stays on this device. No account, no cloud, no one sees it unless you choose to share it.

Your data

Save & share your garage

Your garage lives on this device, in the browser’s own storage. Export it any time as one file — photos included — and import it on another phone or after clearing the browser.

Importing asks first: Merge adds the file’s bikes alongside this garage (same-name bikes are kept as separate builds, labelled “imported”), or Replace wipes this garage and uses only the file’s bikes. Export first if you want a safety copy — either way, you get 12 seconds to undo it.

Receive a handoff

A dyno run sent from Pocket Garage Dyno arrives as a link — tap it and this garage opens with a confirm card. If a messaging app mangled the link, paste the handoff JSON here instead: same check, same confirm, nothing is applied until you say so.

Claim a bike

Buying a bike from another Garage Build Sheets user? They send you a handoff file and a claim code — pick the file here, enter the code, and the bike merges into your garage with its parts, photos, notes, and issues. A wrong code adds nothing.

Example bikes

The three example bikes (Fat Boy, Knucklehead, Softail) are labelled Example. They’re yours to delete whenever you’re done with them.

Start over

Deletes every bike, part, photo, note, and issue on this device. This cannot be undone — export first if you might want it back.

About

Garage Build Sheets — Version 1.0.6. Plain JavaScript, no dependencies, works offline. Photos stay on this device; nothing is uploaded anywhere. Sharing is opt-in only: nothing posts itself.

Prices in the example bikes are example prices only. Specs on the sheets are what you type in — the app never invents a torque, clearance, or factory number.
